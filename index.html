RRS Discovery · North Atlantic

Tracking North Atlantic carbon

Water sampling and ocean profiles to investigate how carbon is distributed through the water column.

Mapping life in the deep ocean

Seafloor mapping, camera surveys and sediment sampling to explore deep-sea habitats.

RRS Sir David Attenborough · Polar waters

Observing a changing polar ocean

Ocean measurements and biological sampling to investigate the relationship between meltwater, ocean conditions and plankton.
